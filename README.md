# Pre-requisites for Onboarding

Before getting started with the WhatsApp API, there are a few essential requirements you’ll need to take care of. These steps help ensure your business complies with Meta’s WhatsApp Commerce Policy and prevent unnecessary delays during onboarding.

If you’re already using the WhatsApp API with another Meta Partner and have a verified Facebook Business Manager, we recommend reviewing our **Migration / Porting** guide to move your setup to **Wbotz** smoothly.

### Website Requirements<br>

A compliant and complete website is mandatory for WhatsApp API onboarding. Please ensure your website meets the following criteria:

* **SSL-secured website**\
  Your website must have a valid SSL certificate and load over HTTPS.
* **About Us page**\
  This page should clearly explain your business, its background, and what you offer.
* **Contact Us page**\
  The legal business name, registered address, and contact number must be listed exactly as they appear in your official documents.
* **Terms and Conditions**\
  Clearly outline the terms under which your services are provided.
* **Privacy Policy**\
  A detailed privacy policy explaining how customer data is collected, used, and protected is required.
* **Copyright information**\
  The footer of your website should include your legal business name.\
  **Format:** © 2025 _Legal Business Name_. All rights reserved.
* **Compliance with Meta policies**\
  Your website and business model must fully comply with Meta’s WhatsApp Commerce Policy.

### Document Requirements

#### **Business Verification Documents** <a href="#h_nlqmcsvaro" id="h_nlqmcsvaro"></a>

Meta requires **any two valid documents** for business verification. These documents must be current, clearly readable, and issued by authorized government bodies.

Accepted documents include:

* Certificate or Articles of Incorporation
* Business registration or license document
* Government-issued tax document\
  _(Self-filed tax documents are not accepted)_
* Business bank statement
* Utility bill\
  _(Utility bills are accepted only to verify business address and phone number. The legal business name must appear on the bill, but it cannot be used for legal name verification.)_

### Phone Number Requirements

The phone number you plan to use with the WhatsApp API must **not already be registered on WhatsApp**. To avoid setup issues, it’s best to use a fresh number.

If you want to convert an existing WhatsApp Business App number to a WhatsApp API number connected to **Wbotz**, you’ll need to either delete the number from WhatsApp or follow the official change-number process before onboarding.

### Final Note

Once these prerequisites are in place, your onboarding with **Wbotz** becomes faster and more seamless. Preparing everything upfront helps avoid rejections and back-and-forth during verification.

If you need assistance at any step, the Wbotz team is always here to help.
